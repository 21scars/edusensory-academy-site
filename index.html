When will the Academy begin?+

January 2027 is the planned launch. Exact start dates, term dates and holidays are awaiting confirmation. December trial lessons and baseline activities are planned, subject to final arrangements.

Who is the Academy for?+

The planned programme serves learners aged 5–14 across Little Explorers (5–7), Creative Thinkers (8–10) and Young Innovators (11–14). Parents and carers join the waiting list and manage enrolment. Joining the list is an expression of interest, not a confirmed place.

When will lessons take place?+

Sessions will be scheduled within these Ghana time (GMT) windows: weekdays, 6:00–8:00 pm; weekends, 2:00–8:00 pm. These are scheduling windows, not the length of a single lesson. Exact pathway and age-group days and times will be shared later. The proposed format remains two 60-minute live sessions per pathway each week.

What equipment will my child need?+

Families will need a suitable device, internet connection and a supportive place to participate. Device, browser and software requirements depend on the pathway and age group and will be confirmed before enrolment. Please wait for guidance before buying equipment or creating tool accounts.

How much does membership cost?+

Each pathway costs GHS 180 per learner per month, payable before the month begins. Approved packages are GHS 680 for one pathway over a four-month term; GHS 500 per month for all three pathways; or GHS 1,900 for all three over a four-month term. Four-month packages are paid in full before the term begins. Offers are per learner and cannot be combined. Joining the waiting list is free and does not reserve a place. This site does not collect payments. For enquiries, contact +233 59 101 7511.

How will Digital Creator use AI?+

AI for Young Learners is the planned Term 3 focus. Activities, tools, account requirements and permissions are subject to academic and safeguarding review. Children will not be asked through this site to create AI accounts. Tool age rules and parent permissions must be checked before use.

Will my child need a ChatGPT account?+

This information website is for parents and carers. It does not provide a child login or teaching platform. Confirmed joining instructions for live lessons and any approved tools will be provided separately.

YOUR INTEREST. YOUR INFORMATION.

Waiting-list privacy.

This site is for parents and carers aged 18+. EduSensory stores the parent/carer name, email, optional phone and country, age-group and pathway interests, preferred contact method and a record of consent submitted through the waiting-list form.

The Academy team uses these details to manage the interest list and follow up about launch and enrolment arrangements. Joining is optional and does not create a learner account, confirm a place or authorise payment. Please do not submit children’s names, dates of birth or sensitive personal information.

Entries are visible only to the authorised Academy administrator. No email or WhatsApp message is sent automatically. For waiting-list questions, corrections or withdrawal, contact the Academy on +233 59 101 7511 or WhatsApp. The waiting-list retention period is awaiting approval before this review site opens publicly.

The site uses technical checks to limit automated submissions. A short-lived hashed network identifier may be used for abuse prevention; raw network addresses are not stored in the waiting list. The hosting provider may process technical access information under its own terms. No advertising trackers have been added by EduSensory.
